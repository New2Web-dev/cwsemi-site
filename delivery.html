Our Delivery Services

At Digisino, we understand the importance of timely and secure delivery of electronic components. We offer flexible shipping options to meet your specific requirements.

For any questions about our delivery services or to request a specific carrier, please don't hesitate to contact us. Our team is ready to assist you with your shipping needs.

Domestic Shipments

We ship packages via any domestic carrier as requested by the customer. Our domestic shipping options include:

  • Standard ground shipping (3-5 business days)
  • Expedited shipping (2-3 business days)
  • Overnight shipping (next business day)

Please contact us for more information about domestic shipping options and pricing.

International Shipments

For our global customers, we offer reliable international shipping services:

  • We ship packages via DHL, FedEx, UPS, TNT, EMS, or any other preferred carrier
  • Shipping cost is based on size, weight, destination, and value of the package (NOT INCLUDING shipping insurance, taxes)
  • We adhere to all export policies and rules. Some products may be ITAR restricted, and export restrictions may cause delays in shipping lead time
  • We also accept the shipping method as requested by the client

Shipping Considerations

When planning your shipments, please consider the following:

  • Customs clearance procedures may add additional time to international deliveries
  • For time-sensitive shipments, we recommend selecting expedited shipping options
  • All shipments include tracking information that will be provided upon dispatch
  • We package all components securely to prevent damage during transit
