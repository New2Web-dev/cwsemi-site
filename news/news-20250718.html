Successful Delivery of STM32F407ZET6 to Brazilian EMS Leader

Digisino Electronics has successfully delivered 36,000 units of the popular STM32F407ZET6 microcontroller to one of Brazil's top three EMS providers. This shipment highlights our strong capabilities in supplying high-demand components to the rapidly growing South American electronics market.

The STM32F series has become increasingly popular in Brazil's industrial automation and IoT sectors due to its powerful ARM Cortex-M4 core and rich peripheral set. Our deep expertise with STMicroelectronics products and established logistics network allowed us to fulfill this large order efficiently:

Key Highlights:

  • Component: STM32F407ZET6
  • Quantity: 36,000 units
  • Origin: STMicroelectronics (Taiwan, China)
  • Destination: Top 3 EMS in Brazil
  • Service: Direct shipment with full certification

Our STMicroelectronics Advantage

Digisino maintains a strategic partnership with STMicroelectronics supply channels, giving us priority access to popular MCUs like the STM32F series. This relationship enables us to provide:

Specialized Capabilities:

  • Consistent supply of STM32F series microcontrollers
  • Competitive pricing for the Brazilian market
  • Fast air shipping to South America
  • Localized technical support
  • End-to-end traceability

This shipment reinforces Digisino's position as a leading supplier to Brazil's electronics manufacturing sector. The STM32F407ZET6's combination of performance and energy efficiency makes it ideal for Brazil's growing industrial automation and IoT applications.

We invite electronics manufacturers in Brazil and worldwide to leverage our expertise in STMicroelectronics products. With direct factory relationships and specialized logistics channels, Digisino can solve your most challenging component sourcing requirements.
