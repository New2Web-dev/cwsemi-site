Digisino Electronics Limited is proud to announce that we have been named to Supply Chain Connect's prestigious "Top 50 Asia-Pacific Distributors List" for 2025. This recognition highlights our significant growth and contribution to the electronics component supply chain across the world region.

The annual ranking by Supply Chain Connect evaluates distributors based on multiple criteria including revenue growth, market reach, customer satisfaction, and innovative supply chain solutions. Digisino secured the 20th position on this year's list.

"This achievement reflects our team's dedication to solving complex supply chain challenges," said our CEO. "Despite market fluctuations, we've maintained double-digit growth by expanding our supplier network and enhancing our value-added services."

The full list can be found on [The Top 50 APAC Distributors List](https://www.supplychainconnect.com/rankings-research/article/55306376/2025-top-asia-pacific-distributors-list).
