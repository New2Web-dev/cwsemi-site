Digisino Electronics Limited is thrilled to announce our strategic component supply partnership with BYD. This collaboration marks a significant milestone in our commitment to delivering robust, high-reliability electronic components to world-leading manufacturing giants.

Our technical and procurement teams worked closely with BYD engineers to streamline the component sourcing lifecycle, ensuring rigorous quality testing, trace-back documentation, and priority allocation for high-demand passive and active semiconductors.

"Partnering with an industry titan like BYD validates our rigorous quality control protocols and agile supply chain capabilities," said our Senior Sales Director. "We look forward to expanding this synergy to support more large-scale green energy and automotive electronics initiatives."

This partnership underscores Digisino’s core competency in delivering secure sources, competitive pricing, and efficient cost-down solutions for tier-one manufacturing partners globally.
